01 · Dashboards & look

Make the wall panel a joy to walk past

The house runs itself; the panel is how it shows its face. These two are about that face — one practical, one pure theatre.

Switchable dashboards

a weekend

One tap — or the time of day — swaps the whole panel. The wall tablet already flips its brightness and state by time; this makes the entire layout follow suit, so you only ever see what matters right now.

  • Daysolar, weather and the day ahead, up front
  • Nighta dim clock and the alarm state — nothing else
  • Guestjust the lights, nothing to fiddle or break
  • Securityevery camera at once, one glance to sweep

Step one shipped — a six-tab, no-scroll panel · see the wall panel page

Still to do: the time-of-day auto-switch and a guest view — and, because the current panel is already built from a theme, an LCARS skin would drop straight in.

A full Star Trek LCARS skin for the panel — the house as a starship's ops console. Tap a system to read it out. It's a mock below, but the numbers are the ones the panel already knows. Pure theatre, entirely doable.

02 · Make it think

Automation with a bit of a brain

Everything's already reporting in — presence, solar, the sun's angle, the alarm's state. These four just join the dots so the house acts on its own, at the right moment.

a weekend

Presence & geofencing

The house disarms as your phone reaches the drive and arms itself when the last person leaves — no fumbling for a fob. The trick is to start the disarm early, so the alarm's slow radio never leaves you waiting at the door.

The indoor half is spec'd — an 11-room mmWave presence design pack

a weekend

Solar-aware automation

The Victron data's already live — so run the big loads when the sun's up and the battery's full. The washing machine, the immersion, the car: all wait for surplus, and hold back the moment you'd be importing. Free energy, spent on purpose.

quick win

Adaptive lighting

Lights that track the sun — warm and low in the evening, bright and cool at midday — with a "last one to bed" sweep that quietly takes the whole house dark behind you.

quick win

Away & holiday mode

A believable, lived-in look while you're away: lights that replay your real evening patterns rather than an obvious timer, tied straight to the alarm's armed state so it arms itself the moment you leave.

03 · Security, levelled up

The alarm's alive — now make it fast and watchful

ambitious

Tone-ACK confirmation

The instant-disarm key-fob relay is already built — but the panel only reports back six to nine seconds later, so the bridge still confirms an arm against a slow clock. The next step gives it an ear: a tiny circuit tapped onto the panel's own sounder, so Home Assistant hears the confirmation beep in milliseconds and only re-fires the fob if it stays silent. Fast and certain.

Spec'd & costed — see the Technical page

05 · In the headset

Roomscribe grows a model library

The Quest 3 mixed-reality mapper already pins real devices to real fixtures and controls them in passthrough. Next it earns a catalogue of real 3D objects, a refine step, and — the big one — models that show live state. Full write-up on the Roomscribe page.

A model library & place-then-refine

ambitious

Swap the placeholder blobs for a library of real smart-home objects — downlighters, pendants, lamps, switches, thermostats, TVs, speakers, fans. Pick from a category, drop it, then nudge, rotate, scale and snap it to the surface.

  • Category picker → exact model → refine
  • Furniture boxes become real pieces the same way
  • Add what the scan missed — a chair, a plant, anything

Sits on the working pin architecture · see the roadmap section

A living 3D twin

a weekend*
